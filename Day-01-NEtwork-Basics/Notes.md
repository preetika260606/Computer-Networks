#1. What is a Computer Network?
Definition

A Computer Network is a collection of two or more devices connected together to communicate and share resources.

These devices may include:

Computers
Mobile phones
Servers
Printers
Routers
Smart TVs
Real-Life Example

Suppose you and your friend are connected to the same Wi-Fi.

Now you can:

Share files
Play multiplayer games
Print documents
Access the Internet

All these devices together form a computer network.

Why doesn't it work if you're on different Wi-Fi networks?
You are at home.

Your friend is in college.

Home Wi-Fi          College Wi-Fi
Your Laptop         Friend Laptop

Now both devices are on different networks.

To communicate, the data must travel through the Internet.

That's why applications like:

WhatsApp
Google Drive
Discord
Zoom

use Internet servers to exchange data.

#3. Components of a Computer Network:Every network has some basic components.

(1) Sender:The device that sends the data.
Example:
Your laptop sends a message.

(2) Receiver:The device that receives the data.
Example:
Your friend's laptop.

(3) Transmission Medium:The path through which data travels.

Examples:Ethernet Cable,Optical Fiber,Wi-Fi

(4) Protocol:A protocol is a set of rules that devices follow while communicating.
Example:
When you speak English with your friend, both of you understand each other because you follow the same language.

Similarly,Computers communicate using protocols.
Examples:
HTTP
HTTPS
TCP
UDP

#4. Types of Networks->

(a)PAN (Personal Area Network):A network used for personal devices within a very short distance.

Range:Around 10 meters

Examples:Bluetooth,Smartwatch connected to phone,Wireless earbuds

(b)LAN (Local Area Network):A network covering a small geographical area.

Range: 1 km

Examples:Home Wi-Fi,School Computer Lab,Office Network
One building or nearby buildings.

Advantages:Fast,Low cost

(c)MAN (Metropolitan Area Network):A network covering a city or metropolitan area.

Examples:A city's cable TV network
University campuses spread across a city

(d)WAN (Wide Area Network):A network covering a large geographical area.

Examples:Internet,Banking network across India,Railway reservation system

The Internet is the world's largest WAN.
